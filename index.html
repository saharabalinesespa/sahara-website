The Sahara Ritual

The Ritual, unfolded.

In Bali, healing is not a service. It is a sequence — pressure, warmth, oil, breath — kept within families and taught by touch.

01

Two hours · seven movements

An uninterrupted sequence, opened with a footbath of warm water and flower petals.

02

Frangipani-infused coconut oil

Cold-pressed virgin coconut oil, steeped with frangipani and a trace of lemongrass.

03

Warmed volcanic basalt

Stones gathered from the island's rivers, warmed to the temperature of resting skin.

04

Balinese palm-pressure lineage

Long, unbroken strokes drawn from a technique passed down within healing families.

05

One therapist, uninterrupted

A single certified Balinese therapist attends the full two hours. No handovers.

06

Jasmine steam finish

Closed with warm towels infused with jasmine, and a moment of stillness.

Frequently asked questions

What areas of Dubai do you cover?

We cover all of Dubai — Dubai Marina, JBR, Palm Jumeirah, Bluewaters, Downtown, Business Bay, DIFC, City Walk, Jumeirah, Umm Suqeim, Al Barsha, Dubai Hills, Arabian Ranches, Meydan, JVC, JLT, Mirdif, Al Quoz, and all gated communities and hotels. If you're unsure, WhatsApp us your location and we'll confirm within minutes.

How far in advance do I need to book?

We accept same-day bookings — simply book via Fresha or WhatsApp us directly. For peak times (weekends, evenings and holidays) we recommend booking 24–48 hours ahead to secure your preferred time slot. Bridal and group bookings require at least 1 week's notice.

What do I need to prepare at home?

Very little. We bring everything — our own professional massage table, fresh towels and linens, all Balinese oils and products, and any specialist equipment for the booked treatments. All we ask is a clear space of approximately 2m × 2m for the massage table, and access to your space a few minutes before the session starts.

Are your therapists male or female? Can men book?

Our team is mostly female, with one male therapist. Everyone is welcome to book — please mention any therapist preference when booking so we can match you appropriately. For couples bookings both guests can be treated at the same time.

Are your therapists certified?

Yes. Every Sahara therapist holds professional certifications in their respective disciplines and has been personally trained and approved by our founder Neny Trisnayanti — who brings 20+ years of five-star hotel wellness experience. All therapists are background-verified and fully insured.
