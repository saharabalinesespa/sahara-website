Deep Tissue Massage

Deep Tissue Massage at home in Dubai

Targets deep muscle layers, relieves chronic tension. Delivered by certified Balinese therapists.

The treatment

What is deep tissue massage?

Slow, firm pressure targeting the deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue to ease chronic tension, knots and stiffness in the back, neck and shoulders.

Pressure: Firm

Your appointment

What happens

  1. Your therapist arrives

    She arrives on time with the table, linens, towels and oils, and sets up quietly.

  2. A short consultation

    Tell her about sensitive areas, health conditions and your preferred pressure.

  3. Your treatment

    Relax while she works, adapting pressure as she goes.

  4. Aftercare

    Drink some water and take it easy — no traffic to face.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Will it hurt?

Deep tissue is firm but should never be painful. Tell your therapist at any time if you want it lighter.

What do you bring?

Everything: a professional massage table, fresh linens and towels, and Balinese oils. You only need a clear space of about 2 m × 2 m.

Can I request a specific therapist?

Yes — mention your preferred therapist when booking and we will try to send her, subject to availability.
