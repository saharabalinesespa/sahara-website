FAQ

Everything you need to know before your first session

Quick answers about booking, preparation, therapists, payment and cancellations.

What areas of Dubai do you cover?

We cover all of Dubai — Dubai Marina, JBR, Palm Jumeirah, Bluewaters, Downtown, Business Bay, DIFC, City Walk, Jumeirah, Umm Suqeim, Al Barsha, Dubai Hills, Arabian Ranches, Meydan, JVC, JLT, Mirdif, Al Quoz, and all gated communities and hotels. If you're unsure, WhatsApp us your location and we'll confirm within minutes.

How far in advance do I need to book?

We accept same-day bookings — simply book via Fresha or WhatsApp us directly. For peak times (weekends, evenings and holidays) we recommend booking 24–48 hours ahead to secure your preferred time slot. Bridal and group bookings require at least 1 week's notice.

What do I need to prepare at home?

Very little. We bring everything — our own professional massage table, fresh towels and linens, all Balinese oils and products, and any specialist equipment for the booked treatments. All we ask is a clear space of approximately 2m × 2m for the massage table, and access to your space a few minutes before the session starts.

Are your therapists male or female? Can men book?

Our team is mostly female, with one male therapist. Everyone is welcome to book — please mention any therapist preference when booking so we can match you appropriately. For couples bookings both guests can be treated at the same time.

Are your therapists certified?

Yes. Every Sahara therapist holds professional certifications in their respective disciplines and has been personally trained and approved by our founder Neny Trisnayanti — who brings 20+ years of five-star hotel wellness experience. All therapists are background-verified and fully insured.

Can I book for a hotel room or villa?

Absolutely — this is one of our most popular setups. We regularly serve guests at hotels across Dubai. Simply provide your hotel name, room number and check-in status when booking. Poolside and cabana sessions are available at many properties — enquire via WhatsApp for arrangements.

How do I pay?

Payment is accepted via Fresha (card on booking), cash on the day, or bank transfer for group/bridal packages. All prices are inclusive of therapist travel within Dubai. A minimum booking value of AED 250 applies per session.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for a minimum of 3 hours' notice for cancellations or reschedules. Cancellations with less than 3 hours' notice may incur a 50% charge. Bridal and group bookings require 48 hours' notice for a full refund. We understand emergencies happen — please contact us as soon as possible and we will always do our best to accommodate you.

Can I request the same therapist each time?

Yes — we always try to honour therapist preferences. When booking via WhatsApp, simply mention your preferred therapist by name. Availability cannot be guaranteed but we will always try our best. Many of our regular clients build a wonderful long-term relationship with their preferred Sahara therapist.

Do you offer gift vouchers?

Yes. Sahara gift vouchers are available for any amount or specific treatment. They make the perfect gift for birthdays, Mother's Day, Eid, and other special occasions. Request one on our gift voucher page or via WhatsApp — we can provide a beautifully designed digital certificate within 24 hours.
