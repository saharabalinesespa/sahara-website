Services and prices

Sahara provides mobile spa, massage, facial, body and nail treatments at your home, hotel or villa in Dubai. All prices are in UAE dirhams (AED), include 5% VAT and include therapist travel within Dubai unless stated otherwise. The price shown at checkout is the price you pay. A minimum booking value of AED 200 applies per session. Treatment descriptions are for information and are not medical advice.

Booking

A booking is confirmed when you receive our confirmation email or message. If a deposit is requested, the booking is held for a short time and is confirmed only once the deposit is received; unpaid bookings are released automatically. We may decline or cancel a booking where the address cannot be reached, safety is a concern, or a treatment is unsuitable for your health; in that case any amount you paid is refunded in full.

Cancellation and rescheduling

Please give at least 3 hours’ notice. Cancelling or rescheduling with less than 3 hours’ notice, or not being available when the therapist arrives, may incur a charge of up to 50% of the treatment price, which may be taken from your deposit. Bridal and group bookings require 48 hours’ notice for a full refund. You can cancel online from the link in your confirmation email, or by WhatsApp. The exact amount refundable is shown on the cancellation page before you confirm.

Payment

Online payments are processed by Network International (N-Genius) on its secure hosted payment page, by Visa and Mastercard cards. Sahara never sees or stores your card number, expiry date or security code. Balances may also be paid by cash or card terminal after the treatment. Full details, including refunds and disputed payments, are in our Payments & Refunds Policy.

Gift vouchers

Vouchers are valid for 12 months from purchase, can be redeemed against any treatment, are not exchangeable for cash and are not refundable once issued, except where required by law. A lost code can be reissued once identity is verified. Treat the code like cash.

Your space and safety

Please provide a clear space of about 2 m × 2 m and access a few minutes before the session. For hotel bookings, confirm that outside service providers are permitted. Tell us about any medical condition, injury, pregnancy or allergy before your booking. Our therapists may stop a session if they feel unsafe or if conduct is inappropriate, and the treatment price remains payable.
