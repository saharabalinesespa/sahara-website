Terms & cancellation policy

The essentials of booking with Sahara.

Details

Cancellation & rescheduling

We ask for a minimum of 3 hours’ notice for cancellations or reschedules. Cancellations with less than 3 hours’ notice may incur a 50% charge. Bridal and group bookings require 48 hours’ notice for a full refund.

Payment & pricing

Payment is accepted via Fresha (card on booking), cash on the day, or bank transfer for group and bridal packages. Prices include therapist travel within Dubai. A minimum booking value of AED 200 applies per session. Prices are indicative and may vary by location and session requirements.

Your space

Please provide a clear space of about 2 m × 2 m and access a few minutes before the session. For hotel bookings, please confirm that outside service providers are permitted in your room.

Health & safety

Please tell us about any medical condition, injury, pregnancy or allergy before your booking. We may advise against a treatment that is not suitable.
