Our Therapists

Meet our professional therapists

Experienced Balinese wellness professionals with international exposure across Indonesia, Bali, Turkey and Dubai.

Each therapist brings individual skills, traditional knowledge and a passion for delivering authentic Balinese spa experiences.

Kadek Yosi Pratiwi

Traditional Balinese Massage

An experienced therapist specialising in authentic traditional Balinese massage. Skilled in combining flowing massage strokes, acupressure and gentle stretching techniques to release tension, improve relaxation and restore body balance.

Putu Desi Arsita Dewi

Therapeutic & Deep Tissue Massage

A dedicated massage professional with expertise in deep tissue techniques and targeted muscle relaxation. Specialises in focusing on tension areas, relieving muscle stiffness and providing personalised treatments based on individual guest needs.

Wayan Sriyani

Relaxation & Stress Relief Massage

A passionate wellness therapist specialising in relaxation treatments. Her gentle yet effective techniques help reduce everyday stress, ease muscle tension and create a deeply relaxing spa experience.

Komang Lisa Sugiantari

Aromatherapy & Holistic Massage

An experienced therapist with a passion for holistic wellness and aromatherapy treatments. Skilled in combining soothing massage movements with essential oils to promote relaxation, improve comfort and enhance overall well-being.

Waryunah Ratna

Muscle Tension & Therapeutic Healing Massage

A knowledgeable therapist specialising in targeted massage techniques and muscle tension relief. Experienced in adapting pressure and treatment methods to individual guest preferences, helping promote physical comfort and relaxation.

Ketut Agus Niawati

Balinese Traditional & Body Relaxation

Specialising in traditional Balinese massage and full-body relaxation treatments. Combines traditional massage knowledge with professional techniques to deliver a personalised and rejuvenating wellness experience.

Kadek Juli Purnami

Body Scrub & Traditional Spa Rituals

A wellness professional with a passion for traditional spa rituals and body treatments. Specialises in body scrubs, exfoliation and relaxing spa experiences designed to leave the skin feeling refreshed and revitalised.

Made Sugiriana

Therapeutic & Full Body Massage

An experienced massage professional specialising in full-body massage and therapeutic relaxation techniques. Focuses on individual guest requirements, combining professional massage skills with attentive care to provide a comfortable and memorable experience.
